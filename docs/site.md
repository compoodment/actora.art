# Site Guide

The main public areas of actora.art are:

- [Home](https://actora.art/) — the interactive Terminal and account entry point.
- [Aurora](https://actora.art/aurora/) — full-page conversations with Aurora.
- [Music](https://actora.art/music/) — public listening plus signed-in libraries, uploads, playlists, and private history.
- [Lab](https://actora.art/lab/) — Space, Liminal, Particles, The Wall, and Actora.
- [Messages](https://actora.art/messages/) — the signed-in full inbox, with the Social footer as its compact companion. Encrypted conversations include a separate private message-request inbox.
- [Account](https://actora.art/account/) — identity, passkeys, recovery status, archive, and deletion.
- [Info](https://actora.art/info/) — public links, contact, and credits.

The shared footer keeps browser back/forward controls, the actoraOS version, account identity, Music, Social, and Aurora close at hand. Social is the compact Messages and notification surface, not a separate people-discovery page or feed. Aurora is not duplicated in the footer while its full page is open.

Some surfaces deliberately keep their own interaction model. Music playback remains a Music feature; Aurora does not start playback or navigate the browser for the visitor. Messages are private and signed-in. Public profiles are opt-in. Lab experiments can have browser-local or account-backed state depending on the experiment.
