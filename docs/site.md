# Site Guide

The main public areas of actora.art are:

- [Home](https://actora.art/) — the interactive Terminal and account entry point.
- [Aurora](https://actora.art/aurora/) — full-page conversations with Aurora.
- [Lab](https://actora.art/lab/) — Space and The Wall.
- [Account](https://actora.art/account/) — identity, passkeys, recovery status, archive, and deletion.
- [Info](https://actora.art/info/) — public links, contact, and credits.

The shared footer keeps browser back/forward controls, the actoraOS version, account identity, Social, and Aurora close at hand. Social is the compact friend-request and notification surface, not a separate people-discovery page or feed. Aurora is not duplicated in the footer while its full page is open.

Some surfaces deliberately keep their own interaction model. Aurora never navigates the browser for a visitor. Public profiles are opt-in. Lab experiments can have browser-local or account-backed state depending on the experiment.

Music and Messages were removed on 2026-11-01. Their old addresses (`/music` and `/messages`) show a short page saying "This has been removed from actora.art."
