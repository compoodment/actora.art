# Site Guide

The main public areas of actora.art are:

- [Home](https://actora.art/) — the interactive Terminal and account entry point.
- [Aurora](https://actora.art/aurora/) — full-page conversations with Aurora.
- [The Wall](https://actora.art/wall/) — a shared ASCII graffiti wall with drawing, erasing, undo/redo, per-visitor tools, budgets, and marks that fade and expire over time.
- [Space](https://actora.art/space/) — a 3D Newtonian orbital sandbox with selectable Solar System and Alpha Centauri simulations, inspection, tracking, time controls, trails, free camera, and signed-in saves. See [space.md](space.md).
- [Account](https://actora.art/account/) — identity, passkeys, recovery status, archive, and deletion.
- [Info](https://actora.art/info/) — public links, contact, and credits, with the privacy and data use link under the credits.

The shared footer keeps browser back/forward controls, the actoraOS version, account identity, and a notifications bell close at hand. The bell opens the compact friend-request and notification surface, not a separate people-discovery page or feed.

Some surfaces deliberately keep their own interaction model. Aurora never navigates the browser for a visitor. Public profiles are opt-in. The Wall and Space can have browser-local or account-backed state.

Lab was removed on 2026-11-01. Old `/lab` addresses show a short removed notice without forwarding. Music and Messages were also removed on 2026-11-01; their old addresses (`/music` and `/messages`) show a short page saying "This has been removed from actora.art."
