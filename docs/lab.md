# Lab

Short descriptions of the experiments and unfinished things at [actora.art/lab](https://actora.art/lab/).

- [Space](https://actora.art/lab/space/) — a 3D Newtonian orbital sandbox with selectable Solar System and Alpha Centauri simulations, inspection, tracking, time controls, trails, free camera, and signed-in saves. See [space.md](space.md).
- [The Wall](https://actora.art/lab/wall/) — a shared ASCII graffiti wall with drawing, erasing, undo/redo, per-visitor tools, budgets, and marks that fade and expire over time.
