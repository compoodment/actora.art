# Music

[Music](https://actora.art/music/) is actora.art's account-only music player. Sign in or register through the homepage terminal; a direct Music link returns you to the same album, playlist, scope, or search after authentication.

## Discover and Library

Discover contains published songs, albums, and public playlists for signed-in visitors. A visitor's Library contains their own or saved collections, while **All tracks** can switch between Library tracks and all published Music.

Albums are public. New playlists are private by default and become discoverable only when their owner makes them public.

## Playback

The player stays available while moving between Discover, collections, tracks, Now playing, Queue, and Lyrics. **Play next** and **Add to queue** work throughout the signed-in player.

Playback also follows you around actora.art through the shared footer. Its compact title strip and square controls expose play/pause, previous, next, seeking, shuffle, and volume without replacing the centered actoraOS home link. Long titles move inside a fixed-width strip instead of widening the footer. Opening full Music creates a new tab, hydrates the full player with the actual current track, and lets every open Actora tab display and control the same playback session while one tab remains the audio owner. If the audio-owning tab closes or disappears unexpectedly, another tab recovers the preserved moment; when the browser requires a fresh gesture, its play control becomes a one-click resume instead. Pressing Play in another tab deliberately moves audio ownership to the tab being used.

Signed-in listening history powers private **Continue listening** and **Recently played** shelves. It can be cleared at any time and is never public profile or activity data.

## Add Songs

Signed-in visitors can add MP3, M4A/AAC, Ogg/Opus, or WAV files. Selected audio, cover art, and details remain in a private review until the uploader explicitly publishes or discards them. Nothing enters the public catalog before **Publish**.

Uploaders can edit or delete their own published songs. Playlist owners can manage their own playlists and change their visibility.

## Sharing and Profiles

Songs, albums, and public playlists can be shared with current friends through Messages. The message keeps a recognizable title if the Music later becomes unavailable, but its active link and playback action disappear.

A profile can show up to eight selected public songs, available albums, and public playlists. Items disappear from the showcase if they are deleted, become unavailable, or a playlist is made private.

Private playlists, unpublished reviews, Library state, and listening history stay private. Music has no public listening history, activity feed, or popularity metrics. See [social.md](social.md) and [privacy.md](privacy.md) for the wider boundaries.

Guests do not receive Music catalog, media, profile-showcase, footer-player, or Aurora Music-tool data. Signing out stops and clears the shared playback session.
