# Privacy statement

Last updated: 11 November 2026

actora.art is a personal, non-commercial website. This statement says which personal data the site processes, why, for how long, who receives it and what your rights are.

## 1. Who is responsible

actora.art is run by computment, a private individual. computment is the controller of the personal data described in this statement. In this statement, "we" and "us" mean computment.

For questions about this statement or to exercise your rights, email [actora@putment.com](mailto:actora@putment.com).

## 2. Summary

- The site shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking services, and does not sell personal data or share it for advertising.
- Your network (IP) address is not stored. The site keeps only a code made from it with a secret key, which cannot be turned back into the address.
- Aurora is an AI. What you send Aurora is passed to Ollama Cloud, mainly in the United States, to write a reply.
- The site runs on a computer that computment owns and operates himself. Visitors reach it through a relay server at Contabo in the European Union. Encrypted backups are stored at Hetzner in Germany.
- You can delete your chats, your memories, your profile and your account yourself at any time, and ask us for anything else by email.

## 3. What we process and why

### 3.1 Visiting the site

When you open a page, your browser sends your network address and ordinary technical details, such as the page requested and the type of browser. The site uses these to deliver the page. Your network address is converted into a code as described in section 3.8 and is not stored.

The site counts requests whose user agent is bot-like, missing or empty. Other requests are not written to an access log. The public /crawlers/ page uses the name that automated programs such as search engines send in their user agent. For each day, it keeps whole-number totals per crawler name and kind of request. These counts contain no network addresses, page addresses, cookies or full user agents. If the counting service is not running, a reduced record of such a visit can end up in the computer's system log instead. It contains the request kind and user agent. The usual address, cookie and page-address fields are removed, but other request headers can remain. The record is removed by the log's normal rotation.

The site's own web server writes error records without the network address, page address or request headers. Error messages are omitted.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in delivering the site and knowing how much it is used (article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 3.2 Using the site as a guest

If you do not have an account, the site gives your browser a guest cookie. It keeps your Aurora conversation together between pages and visits.

**Legal basis:** providing the features you use (article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 3.3 Accounts and sign-in

To create an account you choose a username and register a passkey. We store your username and display name, your role on the site, the public part of each passkey with technical details (such as the type of device, when it was added and last used, and an optional name you give it), and your signed-in sessions with their expiry and last-use times. If you set up a recovery key, we store a one-way check value made from it. The recovery key itself is never stored.

A username and a passkey are needed to create an account. Without them you can still use the site as a guest.

**Legal basis:** providing the account you asked for (article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 3.4 Profiles, guestbooks and friends

If you create a profile, we store its content: the text, images and other files you upload, its design and custom style, and the friends you feature. We remove common location and camera details from pictures and videos you upload to your profile. A profile is private until you publish it. Guestbook comments are stored with their author and the time they were written. We also store friendships, friend requests, the people you block, and your notifications. When a badge is removed, its label and account links can remain in a removed record. Records older than 30 days are removed when badges are maintained or the site starts.

**Legal basis:** providing these features (article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 3.5 Aurora

Aurora is the site's assistant. It is an AI system: its replies are generated automatically and can be wrong.

We store your conversations: each message with the time it was sent, Aurora's replies with a short list of the steps it took (including its own short note on each step), and the name, type and size of any file you attach. When you retry a reply, we keep up to ten versions with their memory changes as part of the chat. The text of an attached file is used for that reply only and is not stored; a keyed fingerprint is kept to recognise the same attachment if you retry.

If you are signed in, we also store your custom instructions and, if memory is switched on, the short facts you asked Aurora to remember, facts it saves on its own and short notes it keeps about you. Aurora can save things it learns about you on its own when that seems useful. You see every save under Aurora's reply and can undo it, and you can see and delete all memories in memory. After reading a file, page or search result in a reply, Aurora will not save on its own in that reply. On its own, Aurora refuses passwords, keys, certain ID numbers, links and sensitive personal data such as health or religion. These checks use word lists and cannot catch every way of expressing sensitive information. You can ask Aurora to remember a health fact about yourself, but requests containing certain credentials or identifiers are refused. Memory can be switched off. You can view, change or delete memories on Aurora's memory page.

A guest's custom instructions are stored in the browser and sent to the site and Ollama Cloud when used for a reply.

Photos and scanned pages are sent to Ollama Cloud for that reply; the site does not keep the images or their separate descriptions, though Aurora's saved reply may describe them.

To write a reply, the site sends Ollama Cloud the conversation (older parts as a summary), Aurora's own instructions, your custom instructions, your display name, username and access role when signed in, up to six automatically selected relevant memories, the date and time in your time zone, the page you are on, the text of attached files, and the results of tools Aurora uses. Those results can also include more of your saved memories when Aurora uses the memory list. Those results can include your username and whether you are signed in, matching parts of your earlier chats, and your notifications. When Aurora searches the web, a short search query is sent to Ollama's web search service, and pages Aurora reads are fetched by Ollama. Your network address is not sent to Ollama.

Please do not share sensitive information with Aurora, about yourself or about others, such as health details, passwords or identification numbers.

**Legal basis:** providing the conversation you start (article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 3.6 The Wall and Space

The Wall is a shared drawing. Anyone can look; drawing needs an account. For each mark we store the account that made it and the network code of the connection it was drawn from. We also store your tool settings and saved colours with your account. Space is at space.actora.art, keeps no saves and needs no account.

**Legal basis:** providing these features (article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 3.7 Reports and moderation

Signed-in visitors can report a profile. A report stores who reported it, the account reported, the reason, an optional note, and a copy of what the profile showed at that moment (without the uploaded files themselves). We use reports to deal with abuse, which can lead to an account being restricted or a profile being hidden. Reports about messages, made before Messages was removed on 1 November 2026, keep the reported message and its attachments under the same rules.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the site safe for its visitors (article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 3.8 Security, limits and abuse prevention

Aurora and the Wall have daily usage limits. To apply them and to block abuse, the site converts your network address into a code with a secret key. The code cannot be turned back into the address. IPv6 addresses are coded by their network (/64), and for guest limits IPv4 addresses are grouped by their /24 range. These codes are stored with usage limits, Aurora conversations, Wall marks and blocks. Access to the site's administration area is recorded in a log, also with a code instead of the address.

Until 2 November 2026 the site stored network addresses directly. They have been removed from the site and from its backups. A small number of remaining copies, in system logs and in short-lived snapshots at our providers, expire on their own by the end of 2026.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and available and in preventing abuse (article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 3.9 Email

If you email actora@putment.com, we use your email address and your message to answer you.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in answering you (article 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, when you exercise your rights, our legal obligation to respond (article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

### 3.10 Error reports

To help us find problems, the site normally sends a small error report when its code fails. It contains a general error description, locations in our own site code, the type of page, the site version and browser family. It does not include what you type, conversations, full page addresses or account identifiers. We keep these reports for up to seven days, never in backups, and only use them to diagnose problems. You can switch error reporting off in account settings; if you are visiting without an account, you can switch it off in this browser with the switch below this statement.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the site working (article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. What is public and who can see it

Published profiles, guestbook comments and visible Wall marks can be seen by anyone. Everything else is private, including Aurora conversations, memories, friendships and blocks. The friends you feature on a published profile are shown there. If someone writes about you on a profile or in a guestbook, you can ask us to remove it at actora@putment.com.

The site's administration tools show account and usage overviews, not the text of Aurora conversations. Reports and their contents are seen only by computment. As the operator of the server, computment has technical access to all stored data and uses it only to run, secure and repair the site.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Signed-in sessions:** 30 days after last use, or until you sign out or the session is revoked.
- **Your account, passkeys, profile and Wall settings:** until you change or delete them, or delete your account.
- **Notifications:** the site keeps the latest 500 in all and drops older ones; notifications about a friend request are removed when either person blocks the other or archives their account.
- **Aurora conversations and custom instructions (signed in):** until you delete them or your account. An empty active conversation is deleted after 6 hours.
- **Aurora conversation (guest):** deleted 3 days after its last message.
- **Aurora memories:** until they expire, you remove them, you delete the conversation they came from, or you delete your account. When Aurora has 50 notes, it removes the oldest unpinned note to make room for a new one.
- **Wall marks and their network codes:** at most 3 days.
- **Network codes on guest usage limits:** until the daily limit resets.
- **Network codes on Aurora conversations and account Wall allowances:** removed after 14 days without use. Guest Wall allowance records are removed when the site starts.
- **Network code on your account's daily limit:** replaced each time you use Aurora and removed after 14 days without use.
- **Network codes in blocks:** at most 90 days, unless an administrator renews the block.
- **Crawler counts:** daily totals for 30 days.
- **Reports:** until a decision is made; closed reports are deleted 180 days after they are closed.
- **Deleted usernames:** reserved for 90 days so nobody else can take them over at once, without any link to the former account.
- **Administration log:** at most 1 year; once it reaches a fixed size, the oldest entries are removed sooner.
- **Aurora instruction editing history:** the editor’s account ID, username, role, reason and time can remain after account deletion. Account administrators can see this history; older entries are removed as the history fills.
- **Relay firewall log:** at most 30 days.
- **Error reports:** up to seven days.
- **Emails:** up to one year after your question has been answered.

When you delete your account, the site removes your account, passkeys, sessions, profile, guestbook, comments you wrote elsewhere, Aurora conversations and memories, friendships, and Wall data at once. Reports keep the copy made when the report was filed, without the link to your account, until the report's own period ends. If you archive your account instead, it is hidden and its data is kept until you restore or delete it, except that pending friend requests and their notifications are removed.

## 6. Backups

To recover from failures, we keep backups:

- nightly copies on the same computer, kept for 7 days;
- copies made before each update of the site: the latest 10, each removed once it is a year old;
- encrypted copies at Hetzner in Germany, made daily and kept as daily copies for 14 days, weekly copies for 8 weeks, monthly copies for 12 months and yearly copies for 3 years. Emails are kept in these backups as yearly copies for up to 5 years.

When you delete something, it disappears from the site at once, but it can remain in a backup until that backup expires: for at most about 4 years (emails: 5). Error reports (section 3.10) are never included in backups. Backups are used only to restore the site after a failure.

## 7. Who receives your data

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising or analytics. Personal data is received only by:

- **Contabo GmbH** (European Union), which provides the virtual server our relay runs on. The relay passes the encrypted (HTTPS) connection through to our server unread: it sees the site name and your network address, but not your requests, and keeps no record of them. Unencrypted requests are redirected to the encrypted address there. Its firewall can record your network address for stray or blocked connection attempts; those records are deleted after at most 30 days.
- **Hetzner Online GmbH** (Germany), which stores our encrypted backups. Hetzner cannot read them.
- **Ollama** (mainly the United States), which receives what Aurora needs to reply, as described in section 3.5. According to Ollama's privacy policy, it processes these requests only to provide its service and does not use them to train models.
- **Authorities**, only when we are legally required to provide data.

## 8. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Aurora's requests to Ollama are processed mainly in the United States, outside the European Economic Area. We have not confirmed that this transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and we have no other transfer safeguards in place with Ollama. The level of data protection in the United States can be lower than in the EU. If you do not want your messages processed in the United States, please do not use Aurora.

All other processing takes place in the European Union.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

The site uses only cookies and browser storage it needs to work. They are not used for tracking or advertising, which is why the site does not ask for your consent and shows no cookie banner.

Cookies:

- **guestId:** keeps a guest's Aurora conversation together. 7 days.
- **authSession:** keeps you signed in. 30 days after last use, or until you sign out or the session is revoked.

Browser storage, kept until you close the tab:

- **actora-terminal-state-v2:** the terminal's recent output and commands.
- **actora-history-current, actora-history-max:** whether back and forward are available in the footer.
- **actora-aurora-guest-preferences-v1:** a guest's custom instructions for Aurora.
- **actora-account-lifecycle-notice:** a one-time notice after you archive or delete your account.

Browser storage, kept until you clear it:

- **actora.wall.toolRail.v1:** your Wall panel width. Older browsers may also have a wall-tool-preference entry; it is no longer updated.
- **wall-info-seen, actora-aurora-hint-seen:** whether you have seen the Wall and Aurora tips.
- **actora-social-leader-v1, actora-social-signal-v1:** lets open tabs share one connection for notifications.
- **actora:error-reports:** whether you switched error reports off in this browser (section 3.10).

Browser storage stays on your device, except that guest custom instructions are sent for Aurora replies when enabled. You can remove it, and the cookies, in your browser's settings.

## 10. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict how we use your data;
- receive your data in a common, machine-readable format;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interest (sections 3.1, 3.7, 3.8 and 3.10).

You can do much of this yourself. In the terminal you can sign out sessions, and in Account you can archive or delete your account. In Aurora you can delete conversations and view, change or clear your memories. You can edit or remove your profile at any time.

For anything else, email [actora@putment.com](mailto:actora@putment.com). We answer within one month. If a request is complex, we may need up to two more months; we will tell you so within the first month. To confirm that a request about an account comes from its owner, we may ask you to confirm it from that account while signed in. We do not ask for a copy of an identity document.

## 11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or where you believe the problem took place.

## 12. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. Daily usage limits apply automatically to everyone in the same way. Blocks and restrictions are set by a person.

## 13. Children

actora.art is not intended for children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 16 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 14. Security

Sign-in uses passkeys, so there are no passwords that can be stolen. Network addresses are stored only as keyed codes. Connections to the site are encrypted, and backups stored outside our own computer are encrypted. The site's pages load nothing from other websites.

## 15. Changes to this statement

We update this statement when the way the site handles personal data changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Earlier versions are available on request.
